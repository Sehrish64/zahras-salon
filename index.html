Good To Know

Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Kya pehle appointment lena zaroori hai?
Haircut, threading aur clean-up ke liye walk-in chalega. Facial, hair colour aur bridal services ke liye WhatsApp (0336-2504823) par pehle book kar lein taake aapko wait na karna pare.
Bridal makeup ka trial hota hai?
Ji haan. Bridal trial Rs 3,000 mein hota hai, aur agar aap 7 din ke andar bridal package book karein to ye raqam package mein adjust ho jati hai.
Salon sirf ladies ke liye hai?
Ji, Zahra's Salon ladies-only studio hai. Facial aur bridal preparation ke liye private rooms available hain.
Payment kaise kar sakti hain?
Cash, Easypaisa aur JazzCash — teeno chalte hain. Bridal packages 30% advance se book ho jate hain.
Home service available hai?
Bridal aur group bookings ke liye Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Gulistan-e-Johar aur qareebi areas mein home service available hai (kam az kam 3 afraad). WhatsApp par quote lein.
Salon kis din band hota hai?
Hum Monday ko band hote hain. Tuesday se Saturday 11 AM – 9 PM, aur Sunday 2 PM – 9 PM khule hain.
